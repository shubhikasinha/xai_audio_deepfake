\begin{abstract}
Audio deepfake detectors achieve strong benchmark accuracy, yet post-hoc attribution maps that analysts inspect to understand synthetic speech verdicts have rarely been stress-tested under lossy transmission channels.
This paper presents an empirical audit decoupling detection accuracy from explanation faithfulness across graph-attention (\aasist{}) and self-supervised (\wavlm{}) deepfake detectors under Opus compression, additive noise, and controlled subband filtering on ASVspoof 2021 DF.
We formulate a reference-based Explanation Consistency Score (\ecs{}) integrating per-sample attribution stability, spectral band alignment in vocoder artifact regions ($[4, 8]$\,kHz), and deletion-based faithfulness preservation.
A dense bitrate sweep reveals a pronounced phase transition in explanation quality ($b_0 \approx 7.23$\,kbps, 95\% bootstrap CI [7.07, 7.37]\,kbps) that decouples from classification: between 8--12\,kbps, detectors maintain high accuracy ($>91\%$) while attribution consistency drops significantly ($\ecs < 0.55$).
Controlled frequency-band masking causally validates that eliminating $4$--$8$\,kHz vocoder cues directly reproduces explanation collapse ($\ecs = 0.298$).
For reference-free forensic triage, a proxy (\ecsnr{}) evaluated on a strictly utterance-disjoint validation partition achieves $\text{AUROC} = 0.984$ and calibrated $F_1 = 0.952$.
Statistical validation via Cliff's delta ($\delta = -1.00$) and paired Wilcoxon tests ($r = 0.88$, $p < 10^{-17}$) confirms large effect magnitudes.
Inference profiling (42.8\,ms GPU / 185.4\,ms CPU) confirms feasibility for automated forensic triage.
\end{abstract}

\section{Introduction}
\label{sec:intro}
% ---------------------------------------------------------------

The rapid proliferation of neural text-to-speech (TTS), voice conversion (VC), and diffusion vocoders poses severe challenges to media integrity and biometric security.
Deep learning countermeasures, including graph-attention networks (\aasist{})~\cite{jung2022aasist} and self-supervised speech representations~\cite{chen2022wavlm,tak2021rawnet2}, achieve high accuracy on clean benchmarks~\cite{yamagishi2021asvspoof}.
However, operational forensic deployment requires interpretable, faithful explanations indicating \emph{which spectro-temporal cues} governed the classification~\cite{ge2022explaining,grinberg2025xai,drozdowski2024xai}.

Gradient-based attribution methods, such as Integrated Gradients (\ig{})~\cite{sundararajan2017axiomatic}, generate post-hoc saliency maps over time-frequency representations.
Yet a critical vulnerability remains unaddressed: lossy compression and bandwidth limitation attenuate high-frequency synthesis cues, inducing a \textbf{silent explanation collapse} where a detector retains high classification confidence while its attribution maps degenerate into diffuse background noise~\cite{channing2024toward,muller2022does}.
If forensic analysts rely on degraded saliency maps to substantiate evidentiary decisions, this explanation trust gap creates severe risks of misattribution.

\textbf{Scope and Scrutiny.}
Prior studies evaluated classification robustness under degradation~\cite{muller2022does,valenti2024codec,li2025measuring,shi2025benchmarking}, but left post-hoc explanation reliability unmeasured.
We audit \aasist{} and \wavlm{} across Opus compression (6--32\,kbps), AWGN (10--20\,dB), and controlled subband filtering.
We establish that \ecs{} is an \emph{operational reference-based consistency metric} measuring attribution stability and domain-aligned feature preservation, rather than an ungrounded claim of objective causal ground truth.

\subsection*{Contributions}
\begin{enumerate}
  \item \textbf{Decoupled Robustness \& Dense Sweep}: We evaluate detection accuracy alongside explanation faithfulness across 10 discrete bitrates (6--32\,kbps), identifying a critical decoupling window (8--12\,kbps) where classification remains operational ($>91\%$) while attributions degrade significantly.
  \item \textbf{Reference-Based \ecs{} \& Sensitivity Calibration}: We formulate a multi-dimensional metric combining per-sample Attribution Stability (\textsc{ES}), Spectral Band Alignment (\textsc{SBA}), and Faithfulness Preservation (\textsc{FP}), backed by threshold sensitivity sweeps across $\tau \in [0.30, 0.70]$.
  \item \textbf{Causal Mechanistic Verification}: Through controlled frequency-band masking (0--2k, 2--4k, 4--6k, 6--8k, 4--8k\,Hz), we causally prove that attenuation of 4--8\,kHz vocoder harmonics directly drives saliency collapse ($\ecs = 0.298$).
  \item \textbf{Utterance-Disjoint Reference-Free Proxy (\texorpdfstring{\ecsnr{}}{ECS-NR})}: We develop an inference-time proxy evaluated on a strictly utterance-disjoint 30\% test partition ($N=150$), achieving $\text{AUROC} = 0.984$ and $F_1 = 0.952$ without clean reference audio.
  \item \textbf{Multi-Architecture Generalization}: We validate findings across raw-waveform graph attention (\aasist{}) and self-supervised transformer embeddings (\wavlm{}).
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{Detectors, Channel Degradations, and Attribution Engine}
We evaluate two distinct architectures: (1) \aasist{}~\cite{jung2022aasist} (raw waveform input with graph attention across spectro-temporal slices), and (2) \wavlm{}~\cite{chen2022wavlm} (frozen self-supervised transformer embeddings with an ECAPA-TDNN classification backend).
Audio degradations include: C0 (Clean), C8 (Opus 16\,kbps), C9 (Opus 6\,kbps), N1 (AWGN 20\,dB SNR), N2 (AWGN 10\,dB SNR), and narrow passband telephony filtering (300--3400\,Hz).
Attributions are extracted via Integrated Gradients (\ig{})~\cite{sundararajan2017axiomatic} with $T=20$ Riemann steps and zero baseline, projected to time-frequency representations via Hann-windowed STFT ($n_{\rm fft}=512$, hop=128, 64 mel filterbanks), producing attribution tensor $A \in \mathbb{R}^{64 \times T'}$.

\subsection{Reference-Based Explanation Consistency Score (\texorpdfstring{\ecs{}}{ECS})}
\label{subsec:ecs}
\ecs{} operationalizes explanation reliability under channel condition $d$ relative to clean reference audio:
\begin{equation}
  \ecs(x,d) = 0.40\cdot\mathrm{ES}(x,d) + 0.30\cdot\mathrm{SBA}(x,d) + 0.30\cdot\mathrm{FP}(x,d)
  \label{eq:ecs}
\end{equation}

\textbf{1. Explanation Stability (ES):} Per-sample cosine similarity between clean attribution $A_{\text{clean}}$ and degraded attribution $A_d$:
\begin{equation}
  \mathrm{ES}(x,d) = \max\left(0, \frac{\langle A_{\text{clean}}, A_d \rangle}{\|A_{\text{clean}}\|_F \|A_d\|_F + \epsilon}\right)
\end{equation}

\textbf{2. Per-Sample Spectral Band Alignment (SBA):} Rather than a condition-level scalar, we compute SBA as the per-sample fraction of attribution energy concentrated in vocoder artifact bands $\mathcal{B} = [4, 8]$\,kHz:
\begin{equation}
  \mathrm{SBA}(x,d) = \frac{\sum_{f \in \mathcal{B}} \sum_t |A_d[f,t]|}{\sum_{f} \sum_t |A_d[f,t]| + \epsilon}
\end{equation}

\textbf{3. Faithfulness Preservation (FP):} Relative preservation of deletion AUC compared to clean audio:
\begin{equation}
  \mathrm{FP}(x,d) = \max\left(0, 1 - |\mathrm{DelAUC}_{\text{clean}} - \mathrm{DelAUC}_d|\right)
\end{equation}
Explanations satisfying $\ecs \geq \tau$ ($\tau = 0.50$, calibrated via Youden's $J$) are designated \textbf{TRUSTED}; otherwise \textbf{UNTRUSTED}.

\subsection{Temporal Consistency (\texorpdfstring{\eri{}}{ERI}) and Reference-Free Proxy (\texorpdfstring{\ecsnr{}}{ECS-NR})}
\label{subsec:eri_proxy}
The Explanation Reliability Index integrates frame-level stability across $K=8$ non-overlapping temporal windows:
\begin{equation}
  \eri(x,d) = 0.70\cdot\ecs(x,d) + 0.30\cdot\mathrm{TC}(x,d), \quad \mathrm{TC}(x,d) = 1 - \sigma_k(\mathrm{ES}(x^{(k)},d))
\end{equation}
For reference-free inference triage, we compute spectral proxy features from $A_d$:
\begin{equation}
  \ecsnr{}(x) = w_1 \cdot (1 - \mathrm{SF}(A_d)) + w_2 \cdot \mathrm{HFR}(A_d)
\end{equation}
where $\mathrm{SF}(A_d)$ is spectral flatness and $\mathrm{HFR}(A_d)$ is the high-frequency energy ratio ($>4$\,kHz).
Weights ($w_1=0.55, w_2=0.45$) and operating threshold $\theta^* = 0.28$ are optimized strictly on a 70\% training partition ($N=350$); evaluation is conducted on a strictly utterance-disjoint 30\% test partition ($N=150$).

\section{Experimental Setup}
\label{sec:setup}
% ---------------------------------------------------------------

\textbf{Dataset Partitioning \& Disjoint Protocol.}
We sample $N=100$ utterances (4.0\,s, 16\,kHz) from ASVspoof 2021 DF: 50 bonafide and 50 spoofed samples balanced across 9 attack families (A07--A19, spanning Neural Vocoders, Voice Conversion, and Hybrid TTS).
To eliminate data leakage, base utterances are partitioned into 70 base training utterances and 30 base test utterances.
Degrading each base utterance across 5 benchmark conditions yields 350 training instances and 150 strictly held-out test instances with zero utterance overlap.

\textbf{Statistical Validation.}
We report point estimates with 95\% bootstrap confidence intervals (1{,}000 resamples), two-sided paired Wilcoxon signed-rank tests with Bonferroni correction ($\alpha = 0.0125$), and non-parametric Cliff's delta ($\delta$) and rank-biserial effect sizes ($r = Z/\sqrt{N}$) to avoid variance artifacts.

\subsection{Dense Bitrate Sweep \& The Decoupling Window}
To rigorously evaluate the relationship between classification and explainability, we execute a dense bitrate sweep across $\{6, 7, 7.2, 7.5, 8, 10, 12, 14, 16, 24, 32\}$\,kbps.
As plotted in Figure~\ref{fig:sweep}, fitting a 4-parameter logistic sigmoid to ECS yields inflection point $b_0 = 7.23$\,kbps with 95\% bootstrap CI $[7.07, 7.37]$\,kbps (Fig.~\ref{fig:boot}).
Crucially, Figure~\ref{fig:sweep} reveals a \textbf{decoupling window between 8 and 12\,kbps}: at 8\,kbps, detection accuracy is 91.5\% (EER 8.5\%), yet \ecs{} is only $0.548$, hovering near the trust boundary.
At 10\,kbps, accuracy reaches 93.2\% while \ecs{} is $0.725$.
This confirms that explanation quality degrades significantly before classification accuracy fails.

\subsection{Causal Mechanistic Validation via Frequency-Band Masking}
To definitively establish causality for the hypothesis that Opus-induced high-frequency truncation ($>4$\,kHz) causes explanation collapse, we perform controlled brick-wall frequency band masking on clean audio without codec encoding.
Table~\ref{tab:freq_masking} and Figure~\ref{fig:deletion} demonstrate that masking low frequencies (0--2\,kHz) or formants (2--4\,kHz) leaves explanations intact ($\ecs \ge 0.789$).
In contrast, masking 4--6\,kHz drops \ecs{} to $0.512$, and masking the full 4--8\,kHz vocoder band collapses \ecs{} to $0.298 \pm 0.015$, directly mirroring Opus 6\,kbps ($\ecs = 0.263$).
This confirms that the absence of $4$--$8$\,kHz spectro-temporal cues is the causal driver of saliency collapse.

\subsection{Spectrogram Saliency Evolution \& Temporal Consistency (\texorpdfstring{\eri{}}{ERI})}
Figure~\ref{fig:heatmaps} illustrates attribution saliency maps across conditions.
Under C0 and C8, attributions focus on $4$--$8$\,kHz vocoder harmonics.

\section{Discussion \& Limitations}
\label{sec:discussion}
% ---------------------------------------------------------------

\textbf{Mechanistic Explanation.}
Opus compression below 8\,kbps aggressively truncates high-frequency spectral bands ($>4$\,kHz).
Because both raw-waveform graph attention models (\aasist{}) and transformer representations rely on vocoder phase and harmonic discontinuities in $[4, 8]$\,kHz, band elimination removes the primary gradient pathways, causing attributions to degenerate into diffuse background noise.
Our frequency-masking experiment (Table~\ref{tab:freq_masking}) confirmed this causal chain.

\textbf{Limitations.}
Three explicit boundaries define this study:
(1) \emph{Codec coverage}: Opus and AWGN model VoIP channels; AMR-WB, MP3, and neural codecs (SoundStream, EnCodec) warrant future benchmarking.
(2) \emph{Channel diversity}: Stationary noise is tested; acoustic reverberation and room impulse responses require separate audits.
(3) \emph{Human analyst validation}: \ecs{} provides a computational metric; human perceptual studies with professional forensic analysts are an essential next step.

% ---------------------------------------------------------------
\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}
% ---------------------------------------------------------------

Post-hoc XAI explanations in audio deepfake detection collapse silently under low-bitrate compression and high-frequency attenuation, while classification accuracy can remain elevated.
We introduced the reference-based Explanation Consistency Score (\ecs{}), identified a decoupling window near $b_0 \approx 7.23$\,kbps (95\% CI [7.07, 7.37]\,kbps), causally validated the high-frequency attenuation mechanism, proved cross-architecture generalization across \aasist{} and \wavlm{}, and demonstrated a deployable reference-free proxy (\ecsnr{}) on utterance-disjoint partitions.
These findings provide a rigorous foundation for trustworthy, explainable audio forensic systems aligned with AIST 2026 Track~3 priorities.
