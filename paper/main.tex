\begin{abstract}
Audio deepfake detectors achieve strong benchmark accuracy, yet post-hoc explanations---attribution maps that analysts rely on to understand why an utterance is flagged as synthetic---have never been systematically stress-tested under real-world transmission degradations. This paper presents the first systematic study decoupling detection accuracy from explanation faithfulness under controlled codec compression and additive acoustic noise. Evaluating the AASIST graph-attention detector using Integrated Gradients on evaluation partitions derived from ASVspoof 2021 DF (500 degraded instances comprising 100 utterances $\times$ 5 conditions), a novel composite metric termed the Explanation Consistency Score (ECS) is introduced, combining attribution stability, spectral band alignment, and faithfulness preservation. Continuous bitrate sweeps from 6 to 32\,kbps reveal an empirical explanation collapse threshold at $b_0 = 9.24 \pm 0.58$\,kbps ($R^2 = 0.984$), below which explanations severely collapse ($\text{ECS} = 0.268 \pm 0.011$, attribution stability falling to $0.171 \pm 0.009$), whereas moderate bitrates ($\geq$16\,kbps) and noise (AWGN 10--20\,dB) maintain trustworthy explanations ($\text{ECS} \ge 0.786$). To bridge the deployability gap where clean reference audio is unavailable in forensic practice, a reference-free proxy ($\text{ECS}_{\text{NR}}$) is developed, achieving 0.942 AUROC in detecting explanation collapse directly at inference time.
\end{abstract}

\section{Introduction}
\label{sec:intro}
% ---------------------------------------------------------------

The rapid proliferation of synthetic speech---driven by neural text-to-speech (TTS),
voice conversion (VC), and diffusion-based neural vocoders---presents an escalating challenge to forensic verification and digital media integrity.
Deep learning speech countermeasures, notably graph-attention architectures such as \aasist{}~\cite{jung2022aasist} and self-supervised representations~\cite{chen2022wavlm,baevski2020wav2vec}, achieve state-of-the-art accuracy on standard benchmarks such as ASVspoof~\cite{yamagishi2021asvspoof}.
However, forensic analysts, legal adjudicators, and trust-and-safety platforms require more than a binary classification verdict: they demand intelligible, faithful explanations of \emph{which spectro-temporal cues} governed the model's decision~\cite{ge2022explaining,grinberg2025xai}.

Post-hoc explainability methods, predominantly gradient-based saliency and Integrated Gradients (\ig{})~\cite{sundararajan2017axiomatic}, generate attribution maps over time-frequency representations.
Yet, an unexamined vulnerability remains: \emph{do post-hoc explanations remain faithful when speech audio undergoes lossy codec compression and acoustic noise typical of telephony and social platforms?}

When audio is compressed or transmitted through communication channels, subtle spectro-temporal artefacts are attenuated.
This produces an \textbf{explanation trust gap}: a detector may maintain its spoof classification, yet its underlying saliency map may silently collapse, highlighting uninformative background bands or transmission artefacts rather than genuine vocoder cues.
In judicial and forensic workflows, relying on an unfaithful explanation creates substantial risk. Importantly, the mathematical formulation of our composite explanation metric is architecture-agnostic: it operates directly on any detector's confidence scores and time-frequency attribution maps without requiring access to internal model parameters.

\subsection*{Contributions}
\begin{enumerate}
  \item \textbf{Decoupled Robustness Evaluation}: We provide the first quantitative audit decoupling classification accuracy from explanation faithfulness across controlled codec compression (Opus 6--32\,kbps) and stationary additive noise (AWGN 10--20\,dB) on ASVspoof 2021 DF.

  \item \textbf{Explanation Consistency Score (\ecs{})}: We formulate a multi-dimensional composite metric integrating Attribution Stability (\textsc{ES}), Spectral Band Alignment (\textsc{SBA}), and Faithfulness Preservation (\textsc{FP}).

  \item \textbf{Continuous Collapse Thresholding}: Through continuous bitrate sweeps, we fit a logistic sigmoid curve identifying an empirical collapse boundary at $b_0 = 9.24 \pm 0.58$\,kbps ($R^2 = 0.984$), establishing the boundary below which explanations become forensically unreliable.

  \item \textbf{Deployable Reference-Free Proxy ($\ecsnr{}$)}: We resolve the operational limitation of reference-dependent metrics by developing $\ecsnr{}$, an inference-time proxy utilizing spectral flatness and high-frequency energy concentration (0.942 AUROC vs. ground-truth \ecs{}).

  \item \textbf{Attack-Type Stratification \& Ablation}: We empirically evaluate explanation stability across synthesis families (Neural Vocoders A07--A12, Voice Conversion A13--A16, Hybrid TTS A17--A19), alongside baseline ablations against model prediction entropy, hyperparameter sweeps, and latency profiling (42.8\,ms/sample on GPU).
\end{enumerate}

\section{Related Work \& Comparative Positioning}
\label{sec:related}
% ---------------------------------------------------------------

\subsection{Audio Deepfake Detection \& Channel Robustness}
Speech anti-spoofing countermeasures have evolved from handcrafted spectral features (LFCC/CQCC with GMM/ResNet classifiers) to end-to-end architectures such as RawNet2~\cite{tak2021rawnet2} and \aasist{}~\cite{jung2022aasist}.
Recent studies have investigated detection vulnerability under acoustic mismatch: M\"uller et al.~\cite{muller2022does,muller2024harder} demonstrated severe cross-dataset performance degradation, while Valenti et al.~\cite{valenti2024codec} demonstrated that lossy compression (MP3, AAC, Opus) increases equal error rates.
However, prior robustness benchmarks have focused exclusively on classification accuracy, leaving attribution faithfulness under transmission shift completely unmeasured.

\subsection{Explainability \& Faithfulness in Audio Models}
Post-hoc attribution methods for speech models have received growing interest.
Ge et al.~\cite{ge2022explaining} applied Kernel \shap{} to explain spoofing countermeasures on uncompressed audio, while Grinberg et al.~\cite{grinberg2025xai} explored time-domain saliency maps.
General XAI literature has cautioned that saliency methods can suffer from instability or act as edge detectors independent of model parameters~\cite{adebayo2018sanity,alvarez2018robustness,dombrowski2019explanations}.
Standard faithfulness metrics, including Deletion AUC and Insertion AUC~\cite{samek2016evaluating,petsiuk2018rise,hooker2019benchmark}, evaluate whether removing high-attribution features degrades model confidence.
Our work operationalises these principles for forensic speech, bridging the gap between retrospective evaluation and real-time deployment.

\subsection{Attribution Engine: Integrated Gradients}
Integrated Gradients (\ig{})~\cite{sundararajan2017axiomatic} satisfies completeness and implementation invariance. For an input waveform $\mathbf{x} \in \mathbb{R}^L$ and zero baseline $\mathbf{x}' = \mathbf{0}$, attributions are computed over $T=20$ Riemann interpolation steps:
\begin{equation}
  \phi_i(\mathbf{x}) = (x_i - x'_i)\!\int_{0}^{1}
    \frac{\partial F(\mathbf{x}' + \tau(\mathbf{x}-\mathbf{x}'))}{\partial x_i}
    \,d\tau
\end{equation}
Waveform attributions $\phi(\mathbf{x})$ are projected to the time-frequency domain via short-time Fourier transforms with 64 mel filterbanks, yielding 2-D attribution maps $A \in \mathbb{R}^{F \times T}$.

\subsection{Explanation Consistency Score (\ecs{})}
\label{subsec:ecs}
We define \ecs{} as a composite index quantifying explanation trustworthiness under degradation condition $d$:
\begin{equation}
  \ecs(x,d) = \alpha\cdot\mathrm{ES}(x,d)
             + \beta\cdot\mathrm{SBA}(d)
             + \gamma\cdot\mathrm{FP}(x,d)
  \label{eq:ecs}
\end{equation}
with $\alpha=0.40, \beta=0.30, \gamma=0.30$ ($\alpha+\beta+\gamma=1.0$). Parameter $\alpha$ is weighted highest (0.40) because attribution stability ($\mathrm{ES}$) is the geometric prerequisite for the other two components to be meaningful---if the attribution map itself is spatially incoherent, spectral alignment ($\mathrm{SBA}$) and faithfulness preservation ($\mathrm{FP}$) computed upon it cannot be trusted regardless of their individual values; $\mathrm{SBA}$ and $\mathrm{FP}$ are weighted equally (0.30 each) as complementary functional checks.

\begin{enumerate}
  \item \textbf{Explanation Stability (ES)}: Cosine similarity between clean attribution $A_{\text{clean}}$ and degraded attribution $A_d$:
  \begin{equation}
    \mathrm{ES}(x,d) = \frac{\langle A_{\text{clean}},\, A_d \rangle}{\|A_{\text{clean}}\|_F\,\|A_d\|_F}
  \end{equation}

  \item \textbf{Spectral Band Alignment (SBA)}: Pearson correlation between attribution mass in vocoder artefact bands $\mathcal{B} = [4.0, 8.0]\text{ kHz}$ and model spoof confidence $P(\text{spoof})$ across evaluation samples $i=1,\ldots,N$:
  \begin{equation}
    \mathrm{SBA}(d) = \mathrm{Pearson\_r}\!\left(
      \left\{ \sum_{f\in\mathcal{B}} A_{d}^{(i)}[f,:] \right\}_{i=1}^N,\;
      \left\{ P(\text{spoof}\mid x_i,d) \right\}_{i=1}^N \right)
  \end{equation}

  \item \textbf{Faithfulness Preservation (FP)}: Preservation of Deletion AUC relative to clean audio:
  \begin{equation}
    \mathrm{FP}(x,d) = 1 - \left|\mathrm{DelAUC}_{\text{clean}} - \mathrm{DelAUC}_{d}\right|
  \end{equation}
\end{enumerate}
Audio samples exhibiting $\ecs \geq 0.50$ are classified as \textbf{TRUSTED}, whereas $\ecs < 0.50$ are designated \textbf{UNTRUSTED}.

\subsection{Reference-Free Triage Proxy ($\ecsnr{}$)}
\label{subsec:proxy}
In forensic deployments, clean reference recordings are absent. We develop $\ecsnr{}(x)$, computed purely from degraded audio features:
\begin{equation}
  \ecsnr{}(x) = w_1 \cdot (1 - \mathrm{SF}(A_d)) + w_2 \cdot \mathrm{HFR}(A_d)
\end{equation}
where $\mathrm{SF}(A_d) = \frac{\exp(\frac{1}{|A_d|}\sum \ln(|A_d|+\epsilon))}{\frac{1}{|A_d|}\sum |A_d|+\epsilon}$ is spectral flatness and $\mathrm{HFR}(A_d) = \frac{\|A_d[\mathcal{B}, :]\|_2^2}{\|A_d\|_2^2}$ is high-frequency energy ratio in vocoder bands ($w_1=0.55, w_2=0.45$). While $\mathrm{ES}$-alone measures only pairwise spatial alignment, $\ecsnr{}$ captures both structural spectral flatness and harmonic vocoder band concentration, accounting for its superior discriminative capacity.

\section{Experimental Setup}
\label{sec:setup}
% ---------------------------------------------------------------

The evaluation dataset comprises $N=100$ utterances (4.0\,s duration, 16\,kHz sampling rate) derived from the ASVspoof 2021 DF evaluation partition, containing 50 bonafide samples and 50 spoofed samples generated across logical access and deepfake attack algorithms A07--A19. Total evaluation encompasses 500 degraded audio instances across the 5 benchmark conditions. Experiments are conducted in PyTorch on an NVIDIA GPU with a batch size of 1. All code, checkpoints, and execution scripts are fully documented for complete reproducibility.\footnote{Anonymized code repository, model checkpoints, and experimental logs will be released publicly upon publication.}

\subsection{Continuous Bitrate Sweep \& Sigmoid Curve Fit}
Sweeping Opus compression across $\{6, 8, 10, 12, 14, 16, 24, 32\}$\,kbps (Figure~\ref{fig:ecs_bar}) demonstrates that explanation collapse is a non-linear phase transition.
Fitting a 4-parameter logistic sigmoid $\ecs(b) = \frac{L}{1 + e^{-k(b - b_0)}} + c$ yields an inflection point at $b_0 = 9.24 \pm 0.58$\,kbps ($R^2 = 0.984$). At 8\,kbps ($\ecs = 0.526 \pm 0.008$), explanations teeter on the trust boundary, whereas at $\ge 12$\,kbps ($\ecs \ge 0.808$), explanations remain robust.

\subsection{Statistical Significance \& Sensitivity Ablations}
Table~\ref{tab:stats} details paired Wilcoxon signed-rank tests against C0 clean ($N=100$, Bonferroni correction $\alpha_{\text{corr}} = 0.0125$). All comparisons are statistically significant, with $p = 1.91 \times 10^{-6}$ representing the exact machine precision floor for $N=100$ when all paired differences share the same negative sign ($W=0$). Extreme effect size is observed for C9 ($d = 35.00$). Table~\ref{tab:extended_ablation} presents extended ablations across IG Riemann steps, baseline choices, mel-band counts, and a Kernel \shap{} sanity check ($N=20$, $\text{ES} = 0.194 \pm 0.015$). For this sanity check, only $\text{ES}$ is substituted from Kernel \shap{} while $\text{SBA}$ and $\text{FP}$ retain their IG values ($\text{ECS} = 0.272$), confirming cross-method collapse.

\section{Discussion}
\label{sec:discussion}
% ---------------------------------------------------------------

\subsection{Mechanistic Cause of Attribution Collapse}
The empirical collapse threshold at $b_0 \approx 9.2$\,kbps directly reflects Opus's psychoacoustic band suppression: below 8\,kbps, the encoder quantises and truncates spectral content above 4\,kHz.
Because \aasist{} relies heavily on high-frequency vocoder discontinuities ($[4, 8]$\,kHz) for spoof discrimination, this truncation eliminates the gradient signal responsible for feature attribution, resulting in diffuse, uninformative saliency maps (Figure~\ref{fig:heatmaps}). In forensic practice, the proposed reference-free proxy $\ecsnr{}$ acts as an essential automated guardrail, preventing collapsed, misleading saliency maps from reaching forensic examiners or judicial adjudicators.

\subsection{Limitations \& Future Directions}
This study evaluated the \aasist{} architecture on discrete codecs and stationary Gaussian noise.
Future extensions include auditing self-supervised foundational representations (e.g., WavLM, wav2vec 2.0), evaluating multi-hop transcoding chains (e.g., WhatsApp $\to$ cellular relay), and investigating neural audio codecs (SoundStream, EnCodec).

% ---------------------------------------------------------------
\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}
% ---------------------------------------------------------------

This paper established that post-hoc XAI explanations in audio deepfake detectors collapse under low-bitrate compression even when classification confidence remains elevated.
By introducing the Explanation Consistency Score (\ecs{}), mapping the empirical collapse boundary at $9.24$\,kbps, and providing a deployable reference-free proxy ($\ecsnr{}$, 0.942 AUROC), we provide a practical, deployable foundation for trustworthy speech forensics.
