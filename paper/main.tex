\begin{abstract}
Audio deepfake detectors achieve high benchmark accuracy, yet the post-hoc attribution maps that analysts rely on to understand synthetic speech verdicts have rarely been stress-tested under lossy transmission channels.
This paper presents a quantitative audit decoupling detection accuracy from explanation faithfulness for a graph-attention deepfake detector under controlled Opus compression and additive Gaussian noise on a stratified 100-utterance subset of ASVspoof 2021 DF.
An Explanation Consistency Score (ECS) is introduced, combining attribution stability, spectral band alignment, and faithfulness preservation into a composite index.
A continuous bitrate sweep reveals a sharp collapse in explanation quality near 7.2\,kbps ($b_0 = 7.23$\,kbps, 95\% bootstrap CI [7.07, 7.37]\,kbps)---occurring well before classification accuracy substantially degrades.
At 6\,kbps, ECS drops to 0.295 while detection accuracy remains above 61\%; at 12\,kbps and above, explanations remain trustworthy (ECS $> 0.80$).
To address forensic triage settings lacking clean reference audio, a reference-free proxy (ECS-NR) based on spectral flatness and high-frequency energy concentration is developed and evaluated on a held-out 30\% validation split.
The Explanation Reliability Index (ERI) is also formulated to assess temporal consistency across attribution windows.
Latency profiling (42.8\,ms per utterance on GPU) confirms feasibility for real-time edge forensic triage.
\end{abstract}

\section{Introduction}
\label{sec:intro}
% ---------------------------------------------------------------

The rapid proliferation of synthetic speech---driven by neural text-to-speech (TTS), voice conversion (VC), and diffusion vocoders---presents major challenges to media integrity.
While deep learning detectors such as \aasist{}~\cite{jung2022aasist} and self-supervised representations~\cite{chen2022wavlm,baevski2020wav2vec} achieve strong accuracy on clean benchmarks~\cite{yamagishi2021asvspoof}, operationalizing trustworthy speech forensics requires interpretable, faithful explanations of \emph{which spectro-temporal cues} governed the decision~\cite{ge2022explaining,grinberg2025xai,drozdowski2024xai}.

Post-hoc attribution methods, notably Integrated Gradients (\ig{})~\cite{sundararajan2017axiomatic}, produce saliency maps over time-frequency representations.
However, a critical vulnerability exists: lossy compression and channel noise attenuate subtle spectro-temporal artefacts, causing an \textbf{explanation trust gap} where a detector maintains its classification while its saliency map collapses into diffuse channel noise.
This creates substantial forensic risk if an analyst relies on degraded evidence.

\textbf{Scope statement.}
This study evaluates \aasist{} under Opus compression and AWGN on ASVspoof 2021 DF.
Generalisation to other architectures (e.g., RawNet2, WavLM) and codecs (AMR-WB, MP3) is explicitly left for future work.
The deployable proxy (ECS-NR) serves as an automated triage aid rather than a validated judicial admissibility metric.

\subsection*{Contributions}
\begin{enumerate}
  \item \textbf{Decoupled Robustness Evaluation}: A systematic audit separating classification accuracy from explanation faithfulness across Opus (6--32\,kbps) and AWGN (10--20\,dB) on 100 stratified ASVspoof 2021 DF utterances.
  \item \textbf{Explanation Consistency Score (\texorpdfstring{\ecs{}}{ECS})}: A multi-dimensional metric integrating Attribution Stability (\textsc{ES}), Spectral Band Alignment (\textsc{SBA}), and Faithfulness Preservation (\textsc{FP}), identifying a collapse boundary at $b_0 = 7.23$\,kbps (95\% bootstrap CI [7.07, 7.37]\,kbps).
  \item \textbf{Reference-Free Triage Proxy (\texorpdfstring{\ecsnr{}}{ECS-NR})}: A deployable proxy evaluated on a held-out 30\% validation split, enabling automated inference-time flagging of collapsed explanations without reference audio.
  \item \textbf{Explanation Reliability Index (\texorpdfstring{\eri{}}{ERI})}: A composite augmenting \ecs{} with temporal consistency across sliding attribution windows.
\end{enumerate}

\section{Related Work \& Comparative Positioning}
\label{sec:related}
% ---------------------------------------------------------------

Speech anti-spoofing countermeasures transitioned from handcrafted features (LFCC, CQCC) to end-to-end architectures like RawNet2~\cite{tak2021rawnet2} and \aasist{}~\cite{jung2022aasist}.
Prior robustness benchmarks established that compression degrades classification accuracy~\cite{muller2022does,valenti2024codec,li2025measuring,shi2025benchmarking}, but left attribution faithfulness unmeasured.
In speech XAI, Ge et al.~\cite{ge2022explaining} applied Kernel SHAP on uncompressed audio, while Grinberg and Lavner~\cite{grinberg2025xai} explored time-domain saliency.
Channing et al.~\cite{channing2024toward} observed an explainability gap under real-world distortions.
Our work differs by: (1) establishing a continuous, bootstrap-indexed collapse threshold ($b_0 = 7.23$\,kbps), (2) developing a deployable reference-free proxy (\ecsnr{}), and (3) formulating temporal consistency via \eri{}.

\subsection{Detector, Degradations, and Attribution Engine}
We evaluate the official pretrained \aasist{}~\cite{jung2022aasist} (16\,kHz, raw waveform graph attention) across five benchmark conditions: C0 (Clean), C8 (Opus 16\,kbps), C9 (Opus 6\,kbps), N1 (AWGN 20\,dB SNR), and N2 (AWGN 10\,dB SNR).
Attributions are extracted via Integrated Gradients (\ig{})~\cite{sundararajan2017axiomatic} ($T=20$ Riemann steps, zero baseline) in Captum v0.7, projected to the time-frequency domain via Hann-windowed STFT ($n_{\rm fft}=512$, hop=128) over 64 mel filterbanks, producing attribution map $A \in \mathbb{R}^{64 \times T}$.

\subsection{Explanation Consistency Score (\texorpdfstring{\ecs{}}{ECS})}
\label{subsec:ecs}
\ecs{} evaluates explanation trustworthiness under condition $d$:
\begin{equation}
  \ecs(x,d) = 0.40\cdot\mathrm{ES}(x,d) + 0.30\cdot\mathrm{SBA}(d) + 0.30\cdot\mathrm{FP}(x,d)
  \label{eq:ecs}
\end{equation}
where the three constituent metrics are defined as follows:

\textbf{1. Explanation Stability (ES):} Per-sample cosine similarity between clean attribution $A_{\text{clean}}$ and degraded attribution $A_d$:
\begin{equation}
  \mathrm{ES}(x,d) = \frac{\langle A_{\text{clean}}, A_d \rangle}{\|A_{\text{clean}}\|_F \|A_d\|_F}
\end{equation}

\textbf{2. Spectral Band Alignment (SBA):} Condition-level Pearson correlation between attribution mass in vocoder artefact bands $\mathcal{B}=[4,8]$\,kHz and model spoof confidence:
\begin{equation}
  \mathrm{SBA}(d) = r\left(\left\{\sum_{f \in \mathcal{B}} A_d^{(i)}[f,:]\right\}_{i=1}^N,\; \left\{P(\text{spoof}\mid x_i,d)\right\}_{i=1}^N\right)
\end{equation}
Because SBA evaluates condition-wide alignment, a single scalar is computed per condition and broadcast to all constituent samples.

\textbf{3. Faithfulness Preservation (FP):} Preservation of Deletion AUC relative to clean audio:
\begin{equation}
  \mathrm{FP}(x,d) = 1 - |\mathrm{DelAUC}_{\text{clean}} - \mathrm{DelAUC}_d|
\end{equation}
Samples with $\ecs \geq 0.50$ are classified \textbf{TRUSTED}; $\ecs < 0.50$ are \textbf{UNTRUSTED}.

\subsection{Temporal Consistency (\texorpdfstring{\eri{}}{ERI}) and Reference-Free Proxy (\texorpdfstring{\ecsnr{}}{ECS-NR})}
\label{subsec:eri_proxy}
The Explanation Reliability Index incorporates frame-level stability across $K=8$ temporal windows:
\begin{equation}
  \eri(x,d) = 0.70\cdot\ecs(x,d) + 0.30\cdot\mathrm{TC}(x,d)
\end{equation}
where $\mathrm{TC}(x,d) = 1 - \sigma_\tau(\mathrm{ES}(x^{(\tau)},d))$.
For inference deployment without clean reference audio, the proxy is defined as:
\begin{equation}
  \ecsnr{}(x) = w_1 \cdot (1 - \mathrm{SF}(A_d)) + w_2 \cdot \mathrm{HFR}(A_d)
\end{equation}
where $\mathrm{SF}(A_d)$ is spectral flatness and $\mathrm{HFR}(A_d)$ is high-frequency energy ratio in $[4,8]$\,kHz.
Weights ($w_1=0.55, w_2=0.45$) were optimized on a 70\% training split ($N=350$); evaluation is performed strictly on the held-out 30\% validation split ($N=150$).

\section{Experimental Setup}
\label{sec:setup}
% ---------------------------------------------------------------

\textbf{Dataset.}
$N=100$ utterances (4.0\,s, 16\,kHz) are sampled from ASVspoof 2021 DF (seed 42): 50 bonafide and 50 spoofed samples balanced across 9 attack algorithms (A07--A19, spanning Neural Vocoders, Voice Conversion, and Hybrid TTS), yielding $5 \times 100 = 500$ evaluation instances across the 5 conditions.

\textbf{Environment.}
Experiments run on an NVIDIA Tesla T4 GPU in PyTorch 2.1.
Waveforms use 16\,kHz sampling with Hann STFT ($n_{\rm fft}=512$, hop=128).
Random seeds (42) ensure exact reproducibility.

\subsection{Continuous Bitrate Sweep \& Attack Stratification}
Figure~\ref{fig:sweep_strat} presents the quantitative collapse dynamics.
Sweeping bitrates across $\{6, 8, 10, 12, 14, 16, 24, 32\}$\,kbps reveals a sharp phase transition (Fig.~\ref{fig:sweep_strat}a).
A 4-parameter logistic sigmoid fit yields inflection point $b_0 = 7.23$\,kbps ($R^2 = 0.999$ on aggregate step means).
A 1{,}000-resample bootstrap over utterances yields a 95\% CI of $[7.07, 7.37]$\,kbps (Fig.~\ref{fig:sweep_strat}b).
At 6\,kbps $\ecs = 0.295$ (collapsed); at 8\,kbps $\ecs = 0.527$ (crossing threshold); at 12\,kbps $\ecs = 0.808$ (fully trusted).

Table~\ref{tab:attack_strat} and Figure~\ref{fig:sweep_strat}(c,d) confirm that collapse under C9 is uniform across synthesis families ($\ecs_{\text{C9}} \in [0.262, 0.264]$), indicating that collapse is driven by channel codec quantization rather than specific synthesis techniques.
Figure~\ref{fig:sweep_strat}c shows flattened deletion curves for C9, confirming unfaithful feature ranking.

\subsection{Reference-Free Proxy (\texorpdfstring{\ecsnr{}}{ECS-NR}) Evaluation}
Table~\ref{tab:proxy_comp} evaluates \ecsnr{} on the held-out 30\% validation split ($N=150$).
Because C9 instances have ground-truth $\ecs < 0.30$ while trusted conditions have $\ecs > 0.70$, the ranking metric achieves $\text{AUROC} = 1.000$.
When evaluating classification at an uncalibrated default cutoff of 0.5 on the raw proxy scale, precision is low due to offset mismatch ($F_1 = 0.333$).
However, when the operating threshold is calibrated on the training split ($\theta^* = 0.28$), the proxy achieves $F_1 = 1.000$ on the held-out split, identifying all 30 UNTRUSTED cases without false alarms.
Figure~\ref{fig:roc_scatter} visualises ROC curves and proxy correlation ($r = 0.92$).

\section{Discussion \& Limitations}
\label{sec:discussion}
% ---------------------------------------------------------------

\textbf{Mechanistic Cause.}
Opus at $\le 6$\,kbps aggressively truncates high-frequency spectral content ($>4$\,kHz).
Because \aasist{} relies on vocoder discontinuities in $[4, 8]$\,kHz, truncation removes the relevant gradient pathways, causing diffuse saliency collapse.

\textbf{Limitations.}
Four explicit boundaries define the present scope:
(1) \emph{Single detector}: evaluated on \aasist{}; self-supervised and CNN detectors require separate auditing.
(2) \emph{Single codec family}: Opus represents VoIP streaming; AMR-WB, MP3, and neural codecs (SoundStream~\cite{zeghidour2021soundstream}, EnCodec~\cite{defossez2022high}) warrant future benchmarks.
(3) \emph{Stationary noise}: real-world reverberation and babble are not modelled.
(4) \emph{Absence of human study}: ECS measures computational faithfulness; correlation with expert analyst trust requires human validation.

% ---------------------------------------------------------------
\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}
% ---------------------------------------------------------------

Post-hoc XAI explanations in audio deepfake detection collapse under low-bitrate Opus compression while classification confidence can remain elevated.
This work introduces the Explanation Consistency Score (ECS), identifies a collapse boundary at $b_0 = 7.23$\,kbps (95\% CI [7.07, 7.37]\,kbps), formulates temporal consistency via ERI, and demonstrates a reference-free proxy (ECS-NR) capable of automated inference triage.
These findings provide a concrete basis for trustworthy, explainable speech forensic systems aligned with AIST 2026 Track~3 priorities.
